  Travel
Venues & Directions

Getting to Laguna Beach

Traffic is unpredictable on the 405 freeway, highway CA-133 (the main access road east to the coast from the freeway) and Pacific Coast Highway (alternate route from adjacent beach cities to the north or south).

Getting around in Laguna Beach

Carpool! Limited free or metered street parking is available in the neighborhood around our venue.

Rideshare on us! Use our Lyft guest code to schedule a ride!

Take the free trolley! Provided by the city of Laguna Beach.

Walk! To many fun locations and beautiful beaches near our venue.

  FAQ

What should I wear?

Festive casual.
Vintage artsy vibes of "Old Laguna" perhaps? Or your best Hawaiian shirt? Colorful and fun with a touch of glamor or novelty.
We hope you'll go explore Laguna and come back to the party as your home base for the afternoon.

We will take a professional group photo with everyone who is present at 3:00!

Comfortable shoes recommended.
Our party will be staged all throughout the yards and cottages, which are quite close together. Not much walking required, but you will be moving around on several surfaces - grassy lawn, sidewalk/asphalt, brick patio, short steps, dance floor!

It's likely to be in the high 70s and sunny. Shaded seating will be available outdoors.
The cottages are air-conditioned and will be open for indoor seating.

If you hang out with us until sunset, bring a hoodie or jacket.
It can be cool or windy at the beach.

Do you have a registry for gifts?

No registry. We treasure our time spent with you. In lieu of traditional gifts, we’d really love a card with a favorite photo, story, memory or recipe to remind us of our connection with you.

What's on the menu?

Catering from our favorite! Urth Caffe

Served buffet-style throughout the day:

  • Brunchy lunch: Quiche, Tamales, Crostini. And the best coffee ever plus a variety of ades, teas and sparkling beverages.
  • Afternoon bites: Mac & cheese, mediterranean platter, Nori plum wraps, turkey burger sliders, chicken & beef skewers. Enjoy a fine cold beer or a glass of wine.
  • Dessert spread: Cakes, cookies & tarts of all sorts! And the best coffee ever.

What can we do around town, before or after the celebration?

We love Laguna for the art and the outdoor beauty - so much to explore within walking or very short driving distance. The city has an excellent Visitor's Guide with maps and itineraries. Here are some of Kevin and Laurel's recommendations:

Nice small waves, semi-pro beach volleyball, quiet and family-friendly areas just off the main beach, rock formations and tide pools.

Our ceremony site on Friday at Crescent Point is just one of a dozen pocket parks and scenic outlooks that run along the coast. There are staircases up to the cliffs from the beach every few blocks, or you can walk out from PCH on the cliff level.

World-class galleries open to the public on almost every block. Our favorite section in North Laguna runs on PCH from Aster (Urth Caffe on the corner) north a few blocks. But you can find them all along PCH south of downtown, and on Forest Avenue downtown. There's also an excellent art museum.

Check out the Pacific Marine Mammal rescue center on Laguna Canyon Road (you drove past it on your way into town on CA-133).

Just a few minutes up the big hill from our cottages/reception! It has a big playground, tennis/pickleball and shade trees.

Right next to the Alta Laguna Park is the Top of the World scenic view and trailhead for several easy-to-hard foothill hikes.

A lovely state park just a few minutes north of Laguna, on both sides of PCH.
