FAQ

What should I wear?

Ceremony and dinner party on Friday evening:

Dressy casual
The ceremony will be outdoors in an area without shade. It may be sunny or breezy.
The average temp at this time of year and hour is 74 degrees, but it can be in the 80s if we are having a hot, dry spell.

Comfortable shoes recommended.
Guests will need to walk on concrete sidewalk or hard gravel paths for about 300 feet from parking/drop off to seating.
We may also walk or stand on the lawn for photos.

Same dress code for our dinner party immediately afterward at the restaurant,
which has an ambience of "fine dining."

Open-house reception on Saturday afternoon:

Festive casual.
Channel the vintage artsy vibes of "Old Laguna" or wear your best Hawaiian shirt.
We hope you'll go explore Laguna and come back to the party as your home base for the afternoon.

We will take a professional group photo with everyone who is present at 3:00!

Comfortable shoes recommended.
Our party will be staged all throughout the yards and cottages, which are quite close together. Not much walking required, but you will be moving around on several surfaces - grassy lawn, sidewalk/asphalt, brick patio, short steps, dance floor!

It's likely to be in the high 70s and sunny. Shaded seating will be available outdoors.
The cottages are air-conditioned and will be open for indoor seating.

If you hang out with us until sunset, bring a hoodie or jacket. It can be cool or windy at the beach.

Travel
Venues & Directions

Getting to Laguna Beach

  • Closest airport: John Wayne SNA ~30 min drive A small regional and low-hassle airport!
  • Also convenient: Long Beach Airport LGB ~45 min drive Also small and low-hassle!
  • Major regional: Los Angeles International LAX ~90 min+ drive

Friday traffic is unpredictable on the 405 freeway, highway CA-133 (the main access road east to the coast from the freeway) and Pacific Coast Highway (alternate route from adjacent beach cities to the north or south).

We strongly recommend that guests plan to arrive in Laguna Beach before 3:00pm Friday to avoid the worst traffic and allow time for parking and checking into accommodations.

Getting around in Laguna Beach

All of our venues are within a 10-minute drive of each other in the city of Laguna Beach.

  • Carpool! Limited free or metered street parking is available in the neighborhoods around our venues. This is the off-season; parking spots can usually be found within 10 minutes.
  • Rideshare on us! Use our Lyft guest code to schedule a ride!
  • Take the free trolley! Provided by the city of Laguna Beach. The route stops a couple of blocks shy of the cross-street for the ceremony, but otherwise it runs directly to our other events and accommodations.
  • Walk! Many fun locations and beautiful beaches and hikes within walking distance from all venues.

About the Ceremony Venue (Friday)
Crescent Bay Point Park

  • 225 Crescent Bay Dr, Laguna Beach 92651
  • Outdoor park atop the cliffs overlooking the ocean
  • Smoking, glass containers, open flames and pets are prohibited.
  • Expect to be in a small open-air amphitheater. It will likely be sunny and breezy.
  • The seating area is not shaded. If it's unusually hot, we will provide hand-held umbrellas for shade.
  • Seating consists of stone benches in the amphitheater. We will provide cushions. Please let us know if you need more supportive seating or space for a wheelchair. Our permit allows for a few seats of this type.
  • Accessibility: Guests will need to walk about 300 feet from the drop-off curb to the seating area. The pathways are paved sidewalks and slightly inclined. No stairs required. We may stand/walk in the grass or adjacent hard gravel paths to take photos after the ceremony. Comfortable shoes are recommended. If this level of activity would be challenging for any guests, please let us know and we will arrange a more comfortable way to get from the curb to the seating area.

  • Or rideshare on us! Use our Lyft code for door-to-door service.

About the Open-House Reception Venue (Saturday)
The Laguna Cottages

  • 255 St Ann's Dr, Laguna Beach 92651
  • Open house with refreshments and activities indoors and outdoors
  • We will have group seating and dance floor set up on the property's parking lot, front yard and adjacent patios.
  • All three cottages on site will be open for climate controlled seating.
  • Smoking and pets are prohibited.
  • Accessibility: Not much walking required but the terrain does vary from grass to inclined sidewalk to brick patio with a couple of shallow steps. Comfortable shoes recommended. If this level of activity would be challenging for any guests, please let us know and we will make sure you have assistance for your comfort.
