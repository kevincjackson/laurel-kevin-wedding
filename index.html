FAQ

What should I wear?

Ceremony and dinner party on Friday evening:

Dressy casual. The ceremony will be outdoors in an area without shade.
It may be sunny or breezy. The average temp at this time of year and hour is 74 degrees, but it can be in the 80s if we are having a hot, dry spell.
Comfortable shoes recommended. Guests will need to walk on concrete sidewalk or hard gravel paths for about 300 feet from parking/drop off to seating. We may also stand in the grass for photos afterward.

Same dress code for our dinner party immediately afterward at the restaurant, which has an ambience of "fine dining."

Open-house reception on Saturday afternoon:

Festive casual. We do hope you'll go explore Laguna and come back to the party as your home base for refreshment and good compnay. But please no flip flops or bathing suits at the reception. Do have fun with a vintage look or your best Hawaiian shirt. Somewhere between "just rolled off the beach" and "garden party."

Comfortable shoes recommended. Our food and activities will be staged all throughout the yards and cottages, but they are quite together. There will not be much walking required, but you will be moving around on several surfaces - grassy lawn, sidewalk/asphalt, brick patio, short steps, dance floor!

It's likely to be in the high 70s and sunny. Shady seating outdoors will be available. The cottages are air conditioned and will be open for indoor seating.

If you hang with us until sunset, bring a hoodie or jacket. It can be cool or windy at the beach.

Venues & Directions

Getting to Laguna Beach

If you are flying in and coming directly to Laguna Beach:

Closest airport: John Wayne SNA ~30 min drive Also small and low-hassle!

Also convenient: Long Beach Airport LGB ~45 min drive Also small and low-hassle!

Major regional: Los Angeles International LAX ~90 min+ drive

Friday traffic is unpredictable on both the 405 freeway, highway CA-133, the main access road to the coast, and the alternate route, Pacific Coast Highway. We recommend planning to arrive in Laguna Beach before 3:00pm to avoid traffic and allow plenty of time for parking and checking into your accommodations.

Getting around in Laguna Beach

Carpool! Limited free and metered street parking is available in the neighborhoods around our venues. This is the off-season, so it should take less than 10 minutes to find a nearby spot.
Rideshare on us! Use our Lyft guest code to schedule a ride right to the park entrance!
Take the free trolley! Provided by the city of Laguna Beach. The route stops a couple of blocks shy of the cross-street for the ceremony, but otherwise it runs directly to our other events and accommodations.
